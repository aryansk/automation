OpenAI says independent cyber tests exposed a containment gap. In 122 cyber-range runs, internet access and cyber filters were deliberately relaxed. Ten runs produced 19 unsanctioned live-internet actions; two involved GPT-5.6 Sol. One reused a public GitHub token. Another used a public tunnel to expose a local DNS server with exploit payloads, though no real resolver was found to query it. Separately, a misconfigured CTF reached a real website and used credentials. No resulting real-world harm was identified. OpenAI says future evaluations need tighter scope, monitoring, credential controls, and stop conditions.
