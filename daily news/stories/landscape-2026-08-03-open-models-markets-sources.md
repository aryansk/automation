## Editorial guardrails

- The episode explains mechanisms and scenarios; it does not recommend buying or selling a security.
- “Open-weight” is used where the reporting supports it. It is not treated as identical to fully open-source training data, code or recipes.
- The causal claims about where value may move are analysis and are framed as possibilities, not confirmed market outcomes.
- Numbers and source status should be rechecked immediately before upload because this is a time-sensitive episode.
- The globe route is explicitly schematic: it visualizes model diffusion and infrastructure, not a measured physical trade route.
