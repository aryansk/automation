  restaurant on Kudrinskaya Square in Moscow, killing at least three and

## 03 — Two workers die in blast at quake-hit Japanese mall

- Event record: `kumamoto-aeon-mall-blast-2026-08-05`
- Trend rank: 3
- Primary reporting: [BBC News — Two workers killed in Aeon Mall blast after re-entering on boss' order](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c24mr09r99eo)
- Independent context: [BBC News — Japan earthquake death toll rises to 34](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cdrv36kv7pzo)
- Key check: two store employees died when an explosion tore through the
  earthquake-hit Aeon Mall in Kumamoto after the magnitude 6.8 quake of
  28 July. Their employer, general goods store Habita, said a boss had asked
  them to re-enter the building to move cash to a safe, and apologised for the
  "loss of two precious lives." Seven of the 38 quake fatalities were from the
  mall. The Short retains the human-interest detail without overstating the
  cause of the explosion.
