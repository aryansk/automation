## 03 — Rare pink-zebra seaweed confirmed as new species

- Event record: `pink-zebra-seaweed-new-species-2026-07-31`
- Trend rank: 3
- Primary reporting: [BBC News](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/czrxepy3vn3o)
- Habitat context: [Natural England — Falmouth Bay to St Austell Bay maerl mapping](https://publications.naturalengland.org.uk/publication/5561639456473088)
- Museum context: [Natural History Museum — New species](https://www.nhm.ac.uk/discover/new-species.html)
- Key check: BBC reports that Prof Christine Maggs identified a pink-striped fragment during a PhD four decades ago; divers later found a sample in Cornwall, DNA testing confirmed a new species, and a specimen entered the Natural History Museum collection. The report gives the scientific name as *Ceramium ruenessianum* and the working common name “pink-zebra weed.” Natural England independently documents the Cornwall maerl-bed habitat and mapping context; it is not used to claim it identified this species.
