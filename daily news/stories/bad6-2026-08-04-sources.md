## 02 — NHS checks miss most high-risk women under 50

- Event record: `uk-breast-risk-screening-gap-2026-08-04`
- Trend rank: 2
- Primary reporting: [BBC News](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ckgv652r625o)
- Research sources: [University of Cambridge Repository — BOADICEA validation](https://www.repository.cam.ac.uk/items/e5c809fa-dd83-4db5-bab5-2926013d0aa0) and [British Journal of Cancer — England pathway analysis](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41416-025-03329-2)
- Key check: BBC reports that current NHS criteria may miss up to 95% of younger women at higher risk and that a broader tool identified eight times as many women in this age group who later developed breast cancer. The Cambridge paper documents BOADICEA’s family-history, questionnaire and genetic inputs; the BJC paper documents current under-50 pathway gaps. This is a reporting summary, not individual medical advice.
