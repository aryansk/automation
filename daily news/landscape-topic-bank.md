# Landscape long-form topic bank

Editorial lane: current-affairs explainers built around one strong question. Each episode is designed for a 16:9 composition with narration, charts, source cards, kinetic type and the atlas globe used when a geographic transition adds meaning.

Refresh current facts, numbers and source links before narration. Topic titles are working titles, not investment advice or a guarantee that every premise remains current.

## Repeatable episode structure

1. A provocative but fact-checkable question.
2. The current event that made the question urgent.
3. A plain-language definition of the system.
4. The money, power or human consequence.
5. What could change next, with uncertainty stated clearly.
