## Reported behavior

The issue requests expansion of a leading `~` in Tuist path literals to the
current user's home directory. The issue author points to
`ProjectDescription/Path.swift` and suggests resolving the path before
constructing the existing relative-to-root representation. The edit notes that
the first proposed implementation may be more complicated than it appears.

## Implemented scope

The change is limited to the current `Path.swift` string-literal initializer:
`~` and `~/...` expand through
`FileManager.default.homeDirectoryForCurrentUser` and become
`.relativeToRoot`; ordinary paths and `//...` retain their existing behavior.
No general shell-style expansion was added.
