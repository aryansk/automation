# Creative brief

The reference is a short philosophical montage: white document-like title cards, dark cinematic inserts, occasional red monochrome passages, archival/charcoal imagery, and an unhurried voice-led arc from hesitation to action. Build a new version from scratch; do not reuse the previous `hyperframes-startagain` composition, timeline, script, audio, or assets.

## Original concept angle

A blank page becomes a dark room, then a doorway: the viewer watches an invisible decision become a physical first mark.

## Visual system

- Warm paper white `#F3F1EB`, charcoal `#111113`, signal red `#E52B2F`, muted slate `#6F747B`.
- White cards use a tiny blue document glyph and understated IBM Plex Mono metadata, inspired by a document interface but with no real brand/logo.
- Cinematic plates use warm amber/cool blue shadows, restrained grain, soft vignettes, and transcript-driven center captions that build word by word with mono body text and signal-red emphasis.
- Red plates are sparse: one silhouette, one sentence, long holds.
- Monochrome plates use original charcoal artwork and soft flicker.
- Typography uses a punch display face for emphatic words, IBM Plex Mono for the timed caption body, and EB Garamond only for the final reflective card.

## Rhythm

`white-hold → white-microcut → warm-cinematic-hold → quick-cut → red-hold → tactile-closeup → archival-hold → white-breath → warm-return → red-peak → open-door-hold → white-close`.

Use hard cuts for the quick edits, gentle crossfades only when the emotional state changes, and a short white flash at the final turn toward hope.

## Audio

- Original offline-generated voiceover via Kokoro, reflective and intimate, not an imitation of the reference narrator.
- Original generated ambient music: sparse felt piano, low room tone, distant tape hiss, and restrained pulse.
- Original/locally resolved SFX: paper tap, keyboard tick, soft whoosh, low impact, and final door/air chime.

## Rights boundary

No reference video download, extracted audio, copied transcript, movie still, creator voice imitation, or borrowed soundtrack is used. All project-bound audio and visual assets are newly generated or synthesized for this draft.
